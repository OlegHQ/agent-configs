# outline-only (outlets that ban or screen AI-written prose)

Deliver `OUTLINE.md` (template `assets/templates/outline-only.md`), `CLAIMS.md` (as the fact sheet), `OPEN.md`, `SUBMIT.md` (title options, one-line pitch of at most 40 words made only of the author's quoted words and facts, bio, form fields), sources list. **No drafted paragraphs.** No "draft" sentences longer than a heading or a bullet fragment; the grader and the author will treat any paragraph of prose as a violation.
Outline: a thesis line, 5-8 numbered sections each with purpose in a few words and the fact IDs it rests on, and exact author quotes from the pack for the places where his voice should carry the piece.
Why: the outlet's rule (quote it in OPEN.md with the access date). The prose pass does not apply (there is no prose); say so. The AI-use statement still goes in SUBMIT.md where the venue asks, saying that the outline and fact sheet were prepared by an AI assistant and the author wrote the text.
Run `check_numbers.py` on OUTLINE.md and `check_citations.py` on the sources.
