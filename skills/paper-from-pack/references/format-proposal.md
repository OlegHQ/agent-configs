# proposal (talk proposal, 1-2 page abstract)

Output: `DRAFT.md` (and `DRAFT.tex`/PDF when the call asks for an IEEE or ACM page format: use that class template and the ieee-conf or acm reference for building).
Shape (adapt to the call's form fields): title; abstract (what the audience will learn, the one number or finding that carries it, 100-200 words); timed outline; why this audience; takeaways the audience can act on; speaker bio (approved only); prior presentation and concurrent-submission statement exactly as the venue asks.
Limits: pages or words in `venue.json`; if the call is a web form, take its field limits (character counts) into SUBMIT.md as the exact fields and check them with `check_limits.py --text` or by counting.
Claims: a proposal promises content; promise only what the data supports. Never claim results not in the pack. State the format (talk, lightning, demo) and duration from the call; unknown values are TO VERIFY.
Concurrent submissions: copy the venue's wording into OPEN.md, and note if a public abstract could break another venue's blind review.
